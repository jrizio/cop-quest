# Cop Quest: Dead Reckoning — Story Bible

A point-and-click police procedural in the style of Sierra's 1993 *Police Quest:
Open Season*: VGA "digitized photo" rooms, a hidden icon bar, points for good
police procedure, and sudden career-ending deaths for bad procedure.

---

## Part 1 — The series so far: where Sonny Bonds left off

The *Police Quest* series followed **Sonny Bonds** of the Lytton (California)
Police Department across three games. This is a recap of that canon. It is
Sierra's material, and it's here only to place our game in the series.

| Game | Year | Sonny's role | What happened |
|------|------|--------------|---------------|
| **PQ1: In Pursuit of the Death Angel** | 1987 | Patrol officer, then undercover narcotics | Sonny works his way up from traffic stops to an undercover sting and brings down drug kingpin **Jessie Bains**, "the Death Angel." Along the way he reconnects with **Marie Wilkans**. |
| **PQ2: The Vengeance** | 1988 | Homicide detective | Bains escapes from jail and hunts the people who put him away, kidnapping Marie. Sonny chases him through Lytton and Steelton, and Bains dies in the final confrontation. Sonny and Marie end up together. |
| **PQ3: The Kindred** | 1991 | Patrol sergeant, pulled back into homicide | Sonny is married to Marie. She is stabbed and left in a coma. A string of ritual killings marked with pentagrams leads to a cult called **the Kindred**, run by **Michael Bains**, Jessie's psychotic brother. Sonny's new partner, **Pat Morales**, turns out to be a dirty cop. In the best ending, Marie wakes from her coma and tells Sonny she's pregnant with their first child. |
| **PQ4: Open Season** | 1993 | *(Sonny doesn't appear)* | The series moved to Los Angeles and a new hero, LAPD homicide detective **John Carey**, chasing a serial killer through South Central and Hollywood. It brought in the digitized photography and icon-bar interface this project copies. |

**Where he left off (1991):** The Bains family is finished. Jessie and Michael
are both gone. The dirty cop inside Lytton PD has been exposed. Marie is awake
and expecting a baby. Sonny is a veteran sergeant who has twice nearly lost his
family to the job. Sierra never made a PQ5 about him. The series went on to
*Police Quest: SWAT*.

### The threads a successor picks up

Each *Police Quest* game is built on the same four things, and *Dead Reckoning*
keeps all four:

1. **The job follows you home.** Every game puts the hero's family at risk or
   under strain.
2. **The rot is inside the department.** PQ3 had Morales. Here it's the
   property room.
3. **Procedure is the puzzle.** You score points for doing it right and get
   killed or fired for doing it wrong.
4. **A seasoned cop starting a new chapter.** Sonny ended PQ3 as a soon-to-be
   father. Our hero starts as a new homeowner with a young family.

## Part 2 — The successor: *Cop Quest: Dead Reckoning*

*Dead Reckoning* has an **original cast and city**, so the game is yours to
build on and share. It's the spiritual next chapter: same genre, same interface,
same tone, and it's set in 1995, two years after *Open Season*.

### Setting

**Mesa Verde, California, March 1995.** A working port city south of LA, with
container docks, a tired marina, union bars, and a police department still
running on green-screen terminals and carbon-copy forms.

### Cast

| Character | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **Det. Gabe Navarro** | You. Mesa Verde PD Homicide, 12 years on the job. He has just moved into a new house, and the bedroom is still full of boxes. |
| **Elena Navarro** | Gabe's wife, an ER nurse at Mesa Verde General. Calling her during the case changes the ending. |
| **Lt. Ruth Kessler** | Homicide commander. She's fair and impatient, and she wants evidence before she asks for a warrant. |
| **Det. Lou Pruitt** | Gabe's partner, eight months from retirement. Your backup, if you remember to call him. |
| **Officer Danny Cho** | Patrol rookie guarding the crime scene. He goes by the book, so badge first. |
| **Dr. Henry Oduya** | Deputy coroner. He's dry, exact, and never wrong about range of fire. |
| **Rosa Delgado** | Crime lab ballistics tech. She runs the casing. |
| **Sgt. Walt Voss** | Property & Evidence sergeant, friendly and helpful. He's quietly selling "destroyed" evidence guns. |
| **Sal Brennan** | Bartender at the Rusty Anchor. He sees everything and says nothing until he sees a badge. |
| **Wendell Pike** | A frightened longshoreman who knows what's in Container 7731. |
| **Kurt Maddox** | Foreman at Pacific Rim Freight. He's the killer. |
| **Tommy Ruiz** | The victim, a union steward with Longshore Local 19. |

### The crime (what really happened)

Sgt. Voss has been signing off seized handguns as "destroyed" and selling them to
Kurt Maddox, who ships them overseas in containers listed as "machine parts."
Tommy Ruiz opened Container PRFU‑7731 and found crates of guns that still had
MVPD evidence tags on them. He told Maddox at the Rusty Anchor that he was going
to the feds. At 2 a.m. Maddox shot Ruiz in his car at Pier 9 with a Glock that
Voss had supplied. Then he put Ruiz's own .38 revolver in his hand to make it
look like a suicide.

**The tell:** a revolver doesn't eject its casings. There's a **9mm casing** on
the asphalt outside the car.

### Act structure and walkthrough

**Act I — Moving Day (Home)**
- Gabe wakes up in the half-unpacked bedroom and the phone rings. Lt. Kessler
  says there's a body at Pier 9: "Looks like a suicide. Go make sure."
- Get dressed from the clothes box. Take your badge and keys. Open the gun
  lockbox with the small key on your ring to get your sidearm and handcuffs.
  Elena's note on the boxes reminds you about dinner at seven.
- In the driveway, open the trunk of your unmarked car to get the field kit
  (gloves, evidence bags, camera, flashlight, radio) and your ballistic vest.

**Act II — Pier 9 (Crime scene, dawn)**
- Show Officer Cho your badge and **sign the crime-scene log**.
- **Photograph** the scene before you touch anything, and **put on gloves**
  before you search.
- Search the body to find Ruiz's union card and a Rusty Anchor matchbook.
- Use the flashlight on the dark asphalt to find the **9mm casing**.
- Cho mentions that a dockhand saw a **black Bronco** tear out of the lot around
  2 a.m.

**Act III — The Investigation**
- **Coroner:** Dr. Oduya finds no soot or stippling, so the shot came from more
  than three feet away. It's homicide. He gives you the 9mm slug.
- **Crime lab:** Rosa runs the casing. It matches a Glock seized in the 1994
  Delacroix case, logged as **destroyed** and signed off by Sgt. W. Voss.
- **Squad room computer:** Run the black Bronco. It's a fleet vehicle for
  Pacific Rim Freight, assigned to foreman **Kurt Maddox**, who has priors for
  assault.
- **Rusty Anchor:** Show your badge. Sal puts Ruiz and Maddox together in a
  shouting match last night: "You open your mouth about the box, you're done."
  Wendell, scared, names **Container 7731, Warehouse 7**. If you accept a drink
  and then drive, your career is over.
- *(Optional)* Call Elena from your desk phone and promise you'll be home by
  seven.
- Bring the evidence to Lt. Kessler. She gets you a **search and arrest warrant**
  for Warehouse 7, and Internal Affairs starts looking at Voss.

**Act IV — Warehouse 7 (Night)**
- Put on your vest and **radio for backup** before you go in.
- Inside, Maddox and Voss are loading the container. **Identify yourself**,
  because opening fire first ends your career. When Maddox goes for his gun,
  **draw and fire** in time. Without the vest, you're dead.
- Cuff Maddox, read him his rights, and open the container. The crates are full
  of MVPD evidence guns, still tagged.

**Epilogue — Home at Seven**
- Maddox is charged with the murder of Tommy Ruiz, and Voss is arrested by IA.
- If you called Elena, she's waiting in the unpacked bedroom. If you didn't,
  there's a plate in the fridge and a note.
- You see your final score and a rank, from "Meter Maid" to "Detective of the
  Year."

### Ways to die or be fired (PQ tradition)

- Drive after drinking at the Rusty Anchor.
- Walk into Warehouse 7 without a vest, without backup, or without your gun.
- Shoot before identifying yourself.
- Say something stupid to an armed suspect.

### Where the story goes next (sequel hooks)

- **Delacroix.** The 1994 gun case was never really closed. Who was Delacroix
  selling to?
- **Elena** is offered a trauma post in LA. Does the family move again?
- **Pruitt's retirement** leaves Gabe training a new partner: Danny Cho, the
  rookie from Pier 9.
- **Overseas buyers.** Container 7731 was the eighth shipment, not the first.
