# Cop Quest — Roadmap

## Done

- **M1 — Walking skeleton.** Scene management, spawns, inventory, hotspots.
- **M2 — The Sierra feel.** PQ4 hidden icon bar, verb cursors (right-click
  cycles), SCI-style message and choice windows, a pixel font, inventory window,
  save / restore (8 slots), a death screen with Restore / Restart / Quit, and
  walk areas with blockers and visibility-graph pathfinding.
- **M3 — The town.** PQ4-style mobile map travel between locations, which
  replaces the top-down driving prototype.
- **M4 — A playable case.** *Dead Reckoning*: 11 locations, 12 characters,
  42 scoring events (142 points), 6 ways to die or be fired, and an epilogue
  with two endings.
- **M5 — Content & polish.** Procedural VGA room paintings, a re-dressed cast,
  synthesized SFX and a title theme, and automated tests.

## Next
